Diabacontrol 💉📱
As the lead developer on Diabacontrol (also known as Diabumvikeli, meaning “protector” in Zulu), I was responsible for building a mobile app designed to support individuals living with diabetes—especially in underserved communities. The app focuses on helping users track key health metrics, access educational resources, and manage their condition more effectively in their daily lives.

I developed both the frontend and backend using React Native with Expo for smooth cross-platform functionality. For backend services, I integrated Firebase, utilizing Firestore for real-time data storage, Firebase Authentication for secure user access, and Cloud Functions to manage data logic and exporting features.

One of the app’s standout features is the Record Data screen, which allows users to input and save their carbohydrate intake, insulin dosage, and blood glucose levels. This data is structured for both personal tracking and research use—giving researchers the ability to export anonymized health records in Excel format.

To support better food choices, I incorporated Google Generative AI to analyze food images and estimate carbohydrate content—making it easier for users to manage their diet accurately using just their phone camera.

I also built a daily diabetes tips feature that dynamically displays helpful advice three times a day (morning, afternoon, and evening), along with push notification reminders to keep users engaged and informed.

Working closely with health professionals and researchers, I ensured the app balanced technical functionality with medical relevance. This project challenged me to think deeply about health accessibility, mobile-first UX, and the integration of AI in healthcare tools—ultimately reinforcing my passion for building impactful digital health solutions.
